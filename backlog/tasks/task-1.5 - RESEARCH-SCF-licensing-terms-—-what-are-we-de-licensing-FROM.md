## Implementation Notes

RESEARCH COMPLETE — findings in research/scf-licensing-terms.md (awaiting owner review).

Gist: SCF license = CC BY-ND 4.0 (Attribution-NoDerivatives) per securecontrolsframework.com/terms-and-conditions — NOT BY-NC-ND, so commercial use of UNMODIFIED SCF is allowed; the restriction is on DERIVATIVES. PERMITS: reproduce/share SCF as-is (incl. commercially, with attribution); adapt for internal use only. FORBIDS (free tier): distributing/selling SCF-derived deliverables (policies, crosswalks) to clients — SCF requires a paid Commercial License (LCP) for derivative content, plus an explicit ban on AI-generation of derivatives. Nothing hard-FORCES de-licensing (buying the LCP legitimizes selling derivatives); de-licensing via clean-room rebuild from public-domain source standards escapes the NoDerivatives clause, the AI ban, ongoing fees, attribution, and tier caps — but the rebuild must be genuinely independent, not reworded SCF (legal-counsel point, UNVERIFIED). Commercial-tier dollar figures UNVERIFIED.
